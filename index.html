LiveSpending tracker

Pocket Cedi

For the end of the month, when you ask yourself where the salary went.

Months went by and I didn’t know why my money was burning. Logging every cedi gave me a direct relationship with my money, so I built the tracker I wanted.

  • Paste a MoMo SMS and the entry fills itself in
  • Budgets follow your pay day, not the calendar
  • Keeps track of who owes you, and who you owe
  • Savings goals with a clay pot that fills up

LiveRunning coach

Run Walk Cycle

From your first minute of running to 30 minutes non-stop, in eight weeks.

I jog in intervals, run five minutes and walk two. I wanted a timer that switches by itself, so I can stop staring at my phone.

  • A voice tells you when to run and when to walk
  • Pocket mode: black screen, taps locked, battery saved
  • Distance and pace with GPS
  • Streaks and personal bests to chase
Open Run Walk Cycle ↗ /run-walk-cycle

LiveProgress photos

Morphē

One photo a day, lined up with yesterday’s, turned into a timelapse of how you’ve changed.

Gym progress is hard to see in the mirror. Morphē puts the days side by side so you can see it.

  • Separate rolls for your face and your full body
  • The outline of yesterday’s photo helps you line up
  • Before-and-after slider, then export a video
  • Photos never leave the phone; optional PIN lock

How I build

No Play StoreEach app is a website you add to your home screen. It opens like any other app.
Works offlineOnce opened, it keeps working with no data bundle.
Your data stays yoursEverything is saved on your phone. No accounts, no servers.
FreeHosted on GitHub Pages, so it costs nothing to use or to run.

I come up with the idea, design the screens, write the copy and test on real phones with friends. I write the code with Claude, an AI assistant, as my coding partner.
